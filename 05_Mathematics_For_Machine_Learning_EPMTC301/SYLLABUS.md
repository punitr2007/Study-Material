# Official Syllabus: Mathematics for Machine Learning

- **Course Code**: `EPMTC301` (B.Tech ECE / ECAM / IT / CS Scheme, NSUT)
- **Course Name**: Mathematics for Machine Learning
- **Course Structure**: `3L-1T-0P` | **Credits**: 4
- **Pre-Requisite**: None

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)
1. To understand the vector space and linear transformation.
2. To know the concept of inner product spaces and norms.
3. To know concept of matrix decomposition.
4. To know concept of vector calculus.
5. To know concept of optimization techniques.

---

## Detailed Unit Contents

### Unit I: Linear Algebra
Vector space, Subspaces, linearly dependent and independent vectors, Basis and dimension of a vector space, Linear Transformation with examples, Rank-Nullity theorem, Matrix representation of Linear Transformation.

### Unit II: Matrix Theory
Inner Product Space, Norms, Orthogonal Component and Projection, Gram-Schmidt process; Properties of Eigen value and Eigen vector, Orthogonal Matrix, Symmetric & Skew Symmetric Matrix, Quadratic form, Positive Definite Matrix, Positive Semi Definite Matrix.

### Unit III: Matrix Decomposition
Singular Value Decomposition (SVD), Pseudo-Inverse, Low Rank Approximation, Polar decomposition, Right Polar Decomposition, Left Polar Decomposition.

### Unit IV: Vector Calculus
Directional derivatives, Hessian, Convex set & function and its properties, Scalar and vector functions, Gradient, Divergence and Curl, Line Integrals, Surface Integrals, Volume Integrals, Green's Theorem, Stoke's Theorem and Gauss Divergence Theorem (without proof).

### Unit V: Optimization
Optimality conditions for unconstrained optimization, Constrained optimization problem, KKT optimality conditions, Convex and Quadratic Programming Problem, Classification problems, Support vector machine (SVM), Steepest descent method.

## Examination Strategy & Weightage Analysis
- **Mid-Semester Weightage**: Unit 1 (45%), Unit 2 (45%), Unit 3 (10%). Focus on Gram-Schmidt process, finding basis and dimension of subspaces, verifying linear transformations, and determining definiteness of quadratic forms.
- **End-Semester Weightage**: Unit 1 (15%), Unit 2 (15%), Unit 3 (25%), Unit 4 (20%), Unit 5 (25%). SVD computation $A = U \Sigma V^T$, pseudo-inverse calculation, KKT conditions for constrained quadratic problems, and SVM primal/dual derivations.
