# Official Syllabus: Signals and Systems

- **Course Code**: `EAEPC302` / `EAEPC05` (B.Tech ECE / ECAM Scheme, NSUT)
- **Course Name**: Signals and Systems
- **Course Structure**: `3-1-0` (L-T-P) | **Credits**: 4
- **Pre-Requisite**: None

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)
After completing the course, students should be able to:
- **CO1**: Understand the concept of signals and systems and their classifications.
- **CO2**: Understand the concept of convolution and its applications.
- **CO3**: Analyze the spectral characteristics of continuous-time and discrete-time periodic and aperiodic signals using Fourier analysis.
- **CO4**: Analyze continuous-time LTI systems in the time domain and s-domain.
- **CO5**: Analyze discrete-time LTI systems in the time domain and z-domain.

---

## Detailed Unit Contents

### Unit I: Signal & System Fundamentals
Concept definition & classification of signals — Continuous time (CT) and Discrete Time (DT) signals, Standard signals — Step, Ramp, Pulse, Impulse, Real and complex exponentials and Sinusoids, Properties of unit impulse in continuous and discrete domain, Concept of orthogonality, Transformation of independent variables, Classification of systems & their properties, Waveform synthesis.

### Unit II: Convolution & LTI Systems
Convolution integrals and its properties, Properties of LTI System, impulse response, step response, convolution sum and its properties, graphical method.

### Unit III: Fourier Analysis of Signals & Systems
Fourier series representation of continuous time and discrete time signals and their properties. Magnitude and Phase spectrum of signals. Continuous time Fourier Transform and its properties, Discrete time Fourier Transform and its properties. Magnitude and Phase representations of frequency response of LTI systems. Introduction to LP/BP/HP/All Pass systems. Relationship between discrete time signals and Sampled continuous time low pass signals.

### Unit IV: Laplace Transform & Continuous-Time LTI Systems
Laplace Transform and its properties, concept of ROC and its properties. Inverse-Laplace transform. Unilateral Laplace transform and its applications. Computation of impulse response, total response (zero state and zero input response) & transfer function using Laplace transform. Stability and causality of continuous-time LTI Systems.

### Unit V: Z-Transform & Discrete-Time LTI Systems
Z-Transform and its properties, ROC and its properties, Inverse Z-transform; Power series expansion and partial fraction expansion. Relationship between Z-transform, Fourier transform and Laplace transform. Stability and causality of discrete-time LTI System. Unilateral Z transform and its applications. Computation of impulse response, total response (Zero state and Zero input response) & Transfer function using Z-Transform.

## Examination Strategy & Weightage Analysis
- **Mid-Semester Exam Weightage**: Unit 1 (45%), Unit 2 (45%), Unit 3 (10%). Focus on graphical convolution, classification of systems (linearity, causality, time-invariance), and step response.
- **End-Semester Exam Weightage**: Unit 1 (15%), Unit 2 (20%), Unit 3 (25%), Unit 4 (20%), Unit 5 (20%). Units 4 & 5 (Laplace and Z-transform) guarantee ~40% of marks with partial fraction inversions and transfer functions.
