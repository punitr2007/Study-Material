# Official Syllabus: Probability Theory and Random Process

- **Course Code**: `EAEPC303` (B.Tech ECE / ECAM Scheme, NSUT)
- **Course Name**: Probability Theory and Random Process
- **Course Structure**: `3L-1T-0P` | **Credits**: 4
- **Pre-Requisite**: None

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)
After completion of this course, students are expected to be able to demonstrate the following knowledge, skills and attitudes:
- **CO1**: Understand probability theory and random variables for the analysis of random phenomena.
- **CO2**: Model probability distributions and functions of random variables.
- **CO3**: Apply moments, characteristic functions and the inequalities.
- **CO4**: Understand random process and its properties, random signal and noise power density spectrum.
- **CO5**: Analyze the LTI system response to random signals and noise processes.

---

## Detailed Unit Contents

### Unit 1: Probability Theory & Random Variables
Introduction to Random Variables, PDF, CDF, Continuous / Discrete / Mix type random variables. Normal, Exponential, Chi-square, Rayleigh, Nakagami-m, uniform distributions etc. Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson distributions. Expectations, Variance, Moments, Characteristics functions.

### Unit 2: Joint Random Variables & Random Vectors
Joint random variables and random vectors, Independent, uncorrelated random variables, Joint Expectation, Covariance, Joint Moments, Joint Characteristic functions, Conditional distributions, Conditional Expectation, Multidimensional Gaussian law.

### Unit 3: Functions of Random Variables & Inequalities
Functions of random variables of the type $Y = g(X)$, $Z = g(X,Y)$ and two functions of two random variables. Inequalities of Chebyshev and Schwartz, Chernoff Bounds, Central limit theorem.

### Unit 4: Random Processes & Ergodicity
Random Processes, First-order Stationary Process, Second-order and Wide sense Stationary Process (WSS), N-th Order and Strict Sense Stationary Process (SSS), Time averages and Ergodicity, Mean Ergodic process, Autocorrelation function and its properties, Cross-correlation function and its properties, Covariance function and its properties, Discrete time processes and sequences.

### Unit 5: Power Density Spectrum & LTI Systems
Power density spectrum (PSD) and its properties, Linear systems with random inputs, Random signal response, Autocorrelation functions of the response, Cross-correlation functions of input and output, Power density spectrum of the response, Spectral representation, Wiener-Khinchine theorem, Gaussian process, White process, Response of LTI systems to White noise.

## Examination Strategy & Weightage Analysis
- **Mid-Semester Weightage**: Unit 1 (50%), Unit 2 (35%), Unit 3 (15%). Focus on PDF/CDF derivations, expectations using MGF, and joint Gaussian distributions.
- **End-Semester Weightage**: Unit 1 (15%), Unit 2 (15%), Unit 3 (15%), Unit 4 (30%), Unit 5 (25%). WSS verification, Wiener-Khinchine theorem, and LTI response to white noise form the major portion of the final exam.
